      Methodology. Transaction facts (value, premium, form of consideration, timing) are sourced from SEC filings, company press releases, and financial press coverage. The Composite Outcome Score is a proprietary weighted model — Strategic Fit (20%), Financial Performance (25%), Market / Analyst Reaction (25%), Synergy Realization (15%), and Premium Discipline (15%) — derived from publicly documented post-close outcomes rather than measured intraday share-price data.
